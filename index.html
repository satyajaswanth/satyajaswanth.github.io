Research

My work lives where memory meets energy. I design non-volatile processors and memory hierarchies — STT-RAM hybrid caches, FRAM main memory, and energy-aware mapping — that let intermittently-powered IoT devices checkpoint their state and resume exactly where they left off after a power failure.

More recently the work has grown up the stack, into dynamic memory management for GPU and RISC-V systems, and outward into control-flow security (return-address-stack protection) and memory-aware on-device inference (TinyML). The common thread is squeezing correctness, performance, and resilience out of severely constrained hardware — where every joule of energy and every byte of retained state has to earn its place.
